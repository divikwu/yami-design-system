# Matcha fixture assets

- `hero.webp`, the three `scene-*.webp` files, and `shortcut-scenes/*.webp` are AI-generated, unbranded editorial visuals for the Topic template.
- `shortcut-scenes/*.webp` are category-specific lifestyle scenes for the six Explore Matcha shortcuts; they intentionally avoid isolated product-packaging shots.
- `products/*.webp` are bundled snapshots of in-stock Yami product images selected on 2026-08-11. Product availability and price may change.
- Generated scenes intentionally avoid logos and packaging; product cards use the corresponding Yami catalog images.
